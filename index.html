FAQ

Common questions

Answers to what EU grant applicants ask most.

Do I need to be an expert in EU grants to use GrantPilot? ▾
No. GrantPilot is designed for founders, not grant consultants. The platform guides you through each step — from finding the right program to writing a complete draft — without assuming prior knowledge of EU funding processes.
Which countries and programs does GrantPilot cover? ▾
GrantPilot focuses on EU-wide programs (Horizon Europe, EIC, InvestEU, LIFE, Digital Europe) and Nordic national programs including Business Finland, Nordic Innovation Fund, and others. Coverage expands as new programs are added.
Does GrantPilot guarantee I'll win a grant? ▾
No — and you should be sceptical of any service that claims otherwise. EU grant competitions are selective by design. GrantPilot helps you find the right programs, write a stronger application, and avoid common mistakes — but the outcome depends on your company's merit and the competition in each call.
How is GrantPilot different from hiring a grant consultant? ▾
Grant consultants typically charge €5,000–€30,000+ per application and work on your behalf. GrantPilot puts the tools in your hands at a fraction of the cost — you remain in control of the content, and the AI handles the heavy lifting of research and drafting.
